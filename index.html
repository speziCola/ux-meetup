About us

One evening, one topic, many perspectives

Every meetup starts with a short talk from real-world practice. After that there is time for discussion and casual conversation over drinks and snacks. Everyone is welcome, and contributions from the community are always encouraged.

Practical
Talks from real projects, from design sprints to UX leadership.
